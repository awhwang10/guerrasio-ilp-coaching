Program Coaching & Accountability Framework

  • Meeting Cadence: Offer bi-weekly to quarterly check-ins based on resident interest and schedule to track question volume via the MKSAP Dashboard.
  • Asynchronous Accountability Option: If preferred, set up periodic email/dashboard progress updates between meetings to maintain accountability.
  • Peer & Group Accountability Option: Encourage forming peer study groups to maintain progress.
